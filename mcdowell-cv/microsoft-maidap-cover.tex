  \item \textbf{Rigorous, vendor-neutral evaluation.} My LLM Eval Harness benchmarks Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT-4o, and Gemini 2.5 Pro on a frozen 2,000-example set, tracking ExactMatch, TokenF1, hallucination rate, latency, and cost with bootstrap confidence intervals and McNemar tests, the kind of measurement discipline a shared AI platform depends on.
\end{itemize}
